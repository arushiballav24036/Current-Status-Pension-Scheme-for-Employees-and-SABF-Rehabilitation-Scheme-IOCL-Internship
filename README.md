# Current-Status-Pension-Scheme-for-Employees-and-SABF-Rehabilitation-Scheme-IOCL-Internship
This project presents two Power BI dashboards developed during my internship at IOCL for monitoring employee-related cases under the EPS95 which is the Pension Scheme for Active and Retired Employees and the Super Annuation Beneficiary Fund (Rehabilitation Scheme). The dashboards transform operational employee data into interactive insights covering case status, location, designation, separation details, pending death cases, settlement progress, succession certificate requirements, extensions, and case aging. The solution enables centralized case monitoring, identification of long-pending cases, and data-driven decision support.

## The Project consists of two major analytical module
1. Pension Scheme for Active and Retired Employees
2. ERO details of Pending Death Cases
# Tools Used
- MS Excel
- Power BI
# Module 1: Pension Scheme for Active and Retired Employees
## Objective
1. To provide a current view of employees under active cases and monitor their status across different locations, designations and employee categories.
2. To help HR teams identify cases requiring attention by analyzing nodal officer status, current status and other case-related categories.
3. To analyze separated employees and their current status, separation reasons and related pension/EPS information.
4. To provide location-wise analysis of active and separated employees, helping identify regions or offices with higher case volumes.
